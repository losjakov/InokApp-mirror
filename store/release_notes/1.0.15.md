## ru

• Исправления и улучшения.

## en
• “I have been here”: mark the monasteries you have visited — a counter, a filter and a mark on the map.
• Quick actions on the app icon: streams, monasteries, map, publications.
• The app will tell you when an important update is out.
• Fixes and improvements.
