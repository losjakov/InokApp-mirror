## ru
• Текст публикаций, обителей и календаря можно выделять и копировать.
• «Назад» из публикации возвращает на то же место списка.

## en
• Text in posts, monasteries and the calendar can be selected and copied.
• Back from a post returns to the same place in the list.
• Home: “New on the site” and “Popular this week” show three posts or videos plus a news item, no repeats.
• A non-default sort is shown above the list; ✕ or 3 hours away from the app resets it.
• “By rating” also weighs the number of ratings; in “News” the order is by date.
• A stream reminder shows a countdown to the start.
• Fixes and improvements.
App Store only, after the second line: • On iPhone: notifications no longer pile up, “Mark as read”, new Lock Screen widgets.
